DevSeva 0.5 — double-click Mac app (formerly Seva Desk)

BEFORE YOU UPDATE
1. Open the old Seva Desk and click Backup database. Save the backup somewhere safe, for example a USB drive.
2. Quit Seva Desk completely, including mobile access.

INSTALL
3. Double-click DevSeva-0.5-Mac-App.zip to extract it (or use the DevSeva.app already extracted in this folder).
4. Drag DevSeva.app into Applications. You can delete the old SevaDesk app from Applications after you have checked the new one. Do not run both at the same time.
5. Double-click DevSeva. Because this is an unsigned prototype, macOS may block it the first time. If so, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and confirm.

No Terminal commands are needed. DevSeva uses the Python/Tk already on your Mac and the SAME database as Seva Desk (Library/Application Support/SevaDeskPrototype). Your existing event, sevas and bookings are kept. The database is upgraded automatically the first time DevSeva opens. After that, keep using DevSeva rather than the old app.

WHAT'S NEW
- Devotees tab: families, members, gotra, rashi, nakshatra, search and booking history.
- One slip per family member, each with that person's own rashi and nakshatra.
- Daily poojas: calendars with chosen weekdays, pooja dates and per-day seva limits.
- Pooja schedule tab with a printable priest sheet.
- Reports tab: collections by payment method, seva and day; unpaid balance; slip-level CSV.
- Archive/restore for events and sevas, and booking search.
- Phones get devotee search, per-member slips and a pooja date picker.
Full details are in DevSeva.app > Contents > Resources > README.md.

FIRST THINGS TO TRY
- Masters: add "Temple daily poojas" as Type Daily, with days "All", and add a seva with a per-day limit.
- Devotees: add your family and its members.
- New booking > Find… > choose the family > Add every member > Save.
- Pooja schedule: check the date, then Print priest sheet.
- Your existing 25 Oct Sri Satyanarayana Pooja bookings appear on the schedule for 2026-10-25.

If DevSeva does not open, it offers to show a diagnostic log (Library/Logs/DevSeva/launch.log). Send a screenshot of that log or of any macOS message for help.
