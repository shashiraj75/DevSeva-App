DevSeva 0.7 — import pooja bookings + event-day counter

BEFORE YOU UPDATE
1. In DevSeva, click Backup database and save the backup on a USB drive.
2. Quit DevSeva.
3. Use the DevSeva.app in this folder (or extract DevSeva-0.7-Mac-App.zip), drag it into Applications and replace the old copy.
   If macOS blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
Your data, staff logins and PINs stay as they are.

FOR THE SRI SATYANARAYANA POOJA (25 OCTOBER 2026)

A. Bring in the bookings you already have (Supervisor or Admin)
   1. Click "Import bookings" at the top and choose "Sri Satyanarayana Pooja · 2026-10-25".
   2. Excel / Google Sheet: "Open Excel / CSV file…". Check the column guesses at the top.
      WhatsApp / PDF / photo of a paper form: copy the text, then "Paste text…".
      Paper forms: "Add a paper form by hand…".
   3. Check the preview:
        Green  = ready
        Yellow = check first (double-click to correct)
        Blue   = will be marked paid
        Grey   = already imported / repeated
      Rows marked Paid in your list are recorded as "Paid before event".
   4. Click "Import N ticked rows". Whenever new bookings arrive, import the updated list again —
      nothing is ever doubled.
   A ready-made sheet for the committee: samples/DevSeva-booking-import-template.xlsx

B. On the day (Reception, Cashier, Supervisor or Admin)
   Laptop: open the "Event-day counter" tab and type the devotee's name, phone, email or booking number.
     - Unpaid (yellow): "Collect cash + print slips" (or "Paid by UPI / card / bank…").
     - Already paid (green): "Print slips only".
     - Confirm when the slips have printed. Reprints are marked REPRINT.
   Phones: Supervisor/Admin starts "Mobile access". Volunteers log in and use Counter mode:
     search > "Money received — mark PAID" > "Send slips to laptop printer".
     The laptop's "Print requests from phones" button shows what to print.
   Each person sees "Collected today by …" for the cash handover.
   The Reports tab has a CASH HANDOVER section per person and payment method.

C. Before the event, please test with 2–3 sample bookings: import, search, print one slip on the real
   printer, and mark one as paid. Then cancel the test bookings (Supervisor) or restore your backup.

ALSO IN 0.7 (from your review)
- Each mobile number can be registered only once. If you enter it again, DevSeva offers to add the person
  to the existing family. Existing duplicates (such as the two "Raviraj Shetty" entries) show a warning
  in the Devotees tab: click "Merge duplicates…" to combine them.
- Email ID field for each family.
- Rashi / Nakshatra: just start typing ("kar" → Karkataka, "ash" → Ashwini / Ashlesha). ↑/↓ and Enter pick.
- New booking: type a name, phone or gotra in "Devotee register" and press Enter or Find….
- Enter = Save / OK, Esc = close, in every window. Cmd+N new booking, Cmd+F search, Cmd+L log out.
- Tooltips on the main buttons; "Log out" button at the top right.
- Phones: iPhone and Android styles, bottom tab bar, and an app icon via "Add to Home Screen".

NOTE: Reception logins can now mark bookings paid (for the event-day counters). Refunds and corrections
still need a Supervisor or Admin.

Sample files to try the import with are in the "samples" folder next to this file.
Full details are in DevSeva.app > Contents > Resources > README.md.
