WHAT'S NEW
• The DevSeva logo and name are at the top left, with your temple's name under them.
• A large maroon banner shows the event name, weekday, date, venue and a countdown
  ("in 39 days", "Tomorrow", "TODAY"). Use "Change event" on the banner to switch events;
  the counter tab follows. Phones show the same event card.
• Masters → "Temple / organisation details…" sets the temple name shown on screen and receipts.
• Copyright is shown in the status bar, login screen, About, receipts, phone page and QR sheet.
  Licence terms: NOTICE.txt.
• "Add every member" is greyed out until you pick a register family with Find….
  For a new devotee, "Add seva line" and then Save booking is all you need.

BEFORE YOU UPDATE
1. In DevSeva, click Backup database and save the backup on a USB drive.
2. Quit DevSeva.
3. Use the DevSeva.app in this folder (or extract DevSeva-0.8-Mac-App.zip).
Your data, staff logins and PINs stay as they are.
4. Open DevSeva, go to Masters → "Temple / organisation details…" and enter your temple's name.

DO THIS ONCE — YOUR DEVELOPER PASSPHRASE (only you should know it)
1. Open DevSeva. Press Command+Shift+D, or click the grey "DevSeva 0.8 · ©…" text at the
   bottom right 5 times quickly.
2. Answer Yes and create a passphrase of at least 10 characters, e.g. three words and a number.
   Write it down somewhere safe. It is saved inside DevSeva.app, not in the temple data.
3. Always do this BEFORE you copy, share or sell DevSeva.
   The standalone builder will not run without it.
   Nobody can change it without knowing the current passphrase.

DEVELOPER TOOLS (same shortcut + passphrase)
• Factory reset — erases ALL data so the copy can go to a new temple:
  bookings, devotees, events, sevas, staff, settings and the audit log.
  - It first asks where to save a final backup.
  - It can also delete the automatic backups and the phone certificate.
  - You must type RESET.
  - DevSeva then opens at first-time setup (temple name + Admin login).
• Reset a customer Admin PIN — for a temple that forgot its Admin PIN. Their data stays.
• Change developer passphrase.
5 wrong passphrases lock the tools for 15 minutes.

GIVING DEVSEVA TO ANOTHER TEMPLE
1. Set your developer passphrase (above).
2. Double-click "Build Standalone DevSeva.command".
3. Give them the ZIP from the "DevSeva Standalone" folder. It carries your passphrase lock
   and needs no Python.
4. On their laptop, DevSeva starts with first-time setup.
(Never copy your own data folder: ~/Library/Application Support/SevaDeskPrototype.)

Everything from 0.7.1 (importing bookings, event-day counter, phone QR login) works as before;
see README.md inside the app for details.
